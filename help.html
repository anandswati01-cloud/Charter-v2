Everything you need to know about booking and flying with SkyVayu.

How Booking Works

SkyVayu connects you directly with aircraft operators — no brokers, no hidden fees. Here's how it works:

  • Enter your departure city, destination, travel date, and passenger count on our homepage.
  • Sign in with your Google account to receive live quotes from verified operators.
  • Review pricing, aircraft type, and operator details on the results page.
  • Confirm your preferred quote and our team will contact you within 2 hours to finalise the booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What routes do you cover?

We cover all major Indian cities and a growing list of international destinations including Dubai, Kathmandu, and the Maldives. If you don't see your route, submit a query and we'll find options for you.

How are prices calculated?

Prices depend on aircraft type, route distance, fuel costs, and operator availability. All quotes are all-inclusive — landing fees and taxes are included.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept NEFT/RTGS bank transfers, UPI, and major credit/debit cards. Payment details are provided after you confirm a quote.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation policies vary by operator. In general, cancellations more than 72 hours before departure attract a 10% fee; cancellations within 72 hours may be non-refundable. Specific terms will be shared at the time of booking confirmation.

Still need help?

Our concierge team is available 7 days a week, 8 AM–10 PM IST. Reach us at support@skyvayu.com or WhatsApp us (number coming soon).
