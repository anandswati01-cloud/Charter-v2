Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 28 May 2025

Information We Collect

  • Account information — name and email address from your Google account when you sign in.
  • Booking queries — departure city, destination, travel date, passenger count, and aircraft preference.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, and browser/device information for analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and respond to your charter flight enquiries.
  • To communicate booking confirmations, updates, and relevant offers.
  • To improve our platform and personalise your experience.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with aircraft operators, payment processors, and service providers under strict data processing agreements.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact us at privacy@skyvayu.com.

Questions about privacy?

Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@skyvayu.com.
