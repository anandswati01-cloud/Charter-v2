From short-hop turboprops to ultra-long-range heavy jets — SkyVayu gives you access to India's most comprehensive charter fleet through our network of verified operator partners.

A note on how we operate
SkyVayu is not a Non-Scheduled Operator Permit (NSOP) holder. We are a technology platform that connects passengers directly with DGCA-licensed NSOP operators across India. Every aircraft you see here is operated by a verified partner operator under their own DGCA-issued permit — giving you full regulatory compliance, competitive market pricing, and access to a varied fleet without broker markups.
