Cookie Policy

How and why SkyVayu uses cookies on this website.

Last updated: 28 May 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us recognise you, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our platform.

Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — Required for the platform to function. These include authentication tokens that keep you signed in. They cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — Anonymised analytics to understand page performance. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Preference cookies — Your theme preference is stored locally using localStorage.

Third-Party Cookies

Our authentication provider (Supabase) and font provider (Google Fonts) may set their own cookies. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from signing in. For more information, visit allaboutcookies.org.

Questions about cookies?
